What the app does

A clearer relationship with your data.

Simple logging, an honest trend, and just enough context to know whether what you are doing is working.

Log any weigh-in

Enter today's weight on a fast keypad, or scroll the date wheel back to record a weigh-in you missed. One entry per day keeps the history tidy.

Edit or delete entries

Your full history lives in the Logbook. Tap any entry to correct its weight or date, or remove it entirely if it was a mistake.

Follow a smoothed trend

A weighted moving average filters out water weight and day-to-day swings, leaving the underlying line your weight is really following.

Know your weekly direction

See at a glance whether your trend is moving up, moving down, or holding steady, with the rate of change per week once there is enough data to be meaningful.

Track a goal weight

Set a target and keep your remaining progress visible on the main screen. Milestones along the way are marked as you pass them.

Expected development Premium

When enough entries exist, the Insights tab extends your recent trend forward and estimates when you would reach your target at the current pace. It is an estimate, not a promise.

BMI and FFMI insights Premium

Add your height, age and body fat percentage to see BMI and FFMI alongside your weight, each shown with its category range.

Built to sit comfortably

Dark and light appearance, metric or imperial units, and a calm layout that puts the number you came for at the top.

Your history, on your account

Sign in with an email address and your entries stay with your account, so a new phone picks up exactly where the last one left off.

How it works

Three steps, then let the trend do the work.

  1. 01

    Log your weight

    Tap the + button in the bottom bar, type your weight, confirm the date, and save. It takes a few seconds.

  2. 02

    Follow your trend

    The Weight tab shows your smoothed trend over the range you choose, so a heavy Monday does not read as a setback.

  3. 03

    Work toward your goal

    Set a target weight and watch the gap close, with expected development and a projected date available in Insights.

Inside the app

Four tabs, no clutter.

Weight for your current number and trend, Insights for deeper analysis, Logbook for your full history, and Settings for your goal, body metrics and account.

Your information

Straightforward about your data.

Weight Arc uses an account, so it does collect information in order to work. Specifically:

  • Your email address and an encrypted password.
  • The weight entries, height, body fat percentage, age and goal weight you enter yourself.
  • Whether your Premium subscription is active.

Your data is stored in the EU and is never sold. There is no advertising in the app, and no location data, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers are collected. You can permanently delete your account and everything in it from inside the app.
