Privacy Policy for Weight Arc

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Weight Arc (“the app”) is developed and operated by Nikolas (“we”). This policy explains what personal data we process, why we process it, and the rights available to you.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address and an encrypted password when you create an account.
  • Health-related information you provide: dated weight entries, height, body fat percentage, age, and goal weight. This information is used only to show your progress and calculate BMI, FFMI, and projections in the app.
  • Subscription status: whether you have an active Premium subscription (through Superwall/Apple). We do not receive your payment details; Apple processes purchases.

We do not collect location data, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and the app does not contain advertising.

2. Purpose and Legal Basis

We process this information only to provide the app's features, including authentication, synchronizing your data across devices, and calculating statistics. The legal bases are performance of our agreement with you (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)) and your explicit consent for health-related data (GDPR Article 9(2)(a)), which you provide by entering that information yourself.

3. Storage and Data Processors

  • Supabase (database and authentication) — your data is stored in the EU (AWS region eu-west-1, Ireland) and transferred using encryption (TLS).
  • Superwall — manages subscription offers and subscription status.
  • Apple — processes subscription payments through the App Store.

We never sell your information and do not share it with anyone other than the processors listed above.

4. Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Account → Delete Account. Deletion is permanent and immediately removes all of your data, including your account, profile, and weight entries, from our systems.

5. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, data portability, and objection. Contact us at valdivian313@outlook.com, and we will respond as soon as possible. You may also lodge a complaint with the Danish Data Protection Agency (datatilsynet.dk).

6. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date above and notify you in the app.

Note for review before launch. This policy is the wording already published for the app; only the product name has been updated. Two points are worth confirming before the App Store submission:

  • The policy does not currently state a minimum age or address use by children. App Store review often expects this.
  • The stated storage region (AWS eu-west-1, Ireland) should be confirmed against the actual Supabase project settings.

Remove this note once both points have been checked. It is the only added content on this page.
