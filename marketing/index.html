Product overview

A weight tracker that shows the direction, not just the number.

Weight Arc is an iPhone app for logging your weight and understanding what it is actually doing over time. Daily weigh-ins bounce around for reasons that have nothing to do with progress; the app smooths that into a trend line and measures your progress against it.

How it is organised

Four tabs.

Everything in the app lives in one of these.

Weight

Your latest weigh-in, the smoothed trend graph over a range you pick, and how far you are from your goal.

Insights Premium

Deeper analysis: median weight, expected development, a projected goal date, and your BMI and FFMI with category ranges.

Logbook

Every entry you have made, newest first. Tap one to correct its weight or date, or delete it.

Settings

Your goal weight, body metrics, units and appearance, your account, and your subscription.

Worth being clear about

What the app does not claim.

The trend and projections are arithmetic on the weights you enter. A projection extends your recent rate forward and assumes nothing changes, which is rarely true — it is an estimate, not a prediction of what will happen.

Nothing in the app is medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and BMI and FFMI are rough population measures rather than an assessment of your health. Talk to a doctor before making decisions about your weight, diet or health.

Projections and weekly rates only appear once there are enough entries over enough time for the number to mean anything.

Your information

What is collected.

An email address and password, the weight entries and body metrics you enter yourself, and whether your subscription is active. Stored in the EU, never sold, no advertising and no advertising identifiers. You can permanently delete your account and all of it from inside the app.
