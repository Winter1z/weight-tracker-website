Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

These steps use the labels currently shown in the app.

How do I log a weight entry?
  1. Tap the + button in the centre of the bottom tab bar.
  2. Enter your weight using the keypad.
  3. Check the date shown on the date wheel.
  4. Tap Save.

The keypad accepts up to three digits and one decimal, and uses whichever unit you have selected under Settings → Preferences.

How do I add a weigh-in from an earlier date?
  1. Tap + in the bottom tab bar.
  2. Enter the weight.
  3. Scroll the day, month and year wheels to the date you want.
  4. Tap Save.

Future dates cannot be selected. The app keeps one entry per day, so saving onto a date that already has an entry replaces that entry.

How do I edit or delete an entry?

Open the Logbook tab to see your full history.

  • To edit: tap the entry, change the weight or the date, then tap Save.
  • To delete: tap the trash icon beside the entry and confirm.

If you move an entry onto a date that already has one, the app will tell you a weight entry already exists for that date. Delete or edit the existing entry first.

How do I set or change my goal weight?
  1. Open the Settings tab.
  2. Tap Goal.
  3. Enter your new target weight and save.

Clear the goal field and save if you no longer want to track a target.

How do I change units or switch between dark and light?
  1. Open the Settings tab.
  2. Tap Preferences.
  3. Choose your appearance and your metric or imperial units.

Units affect display only — your history is preserved and converted, not rewritten.

How do I add my height, age or body fat percentage?
  1. Open the Settings tab.
  2. Tap Body Metrics.
  3. Enter your height, body fat percentage and age, then save.

These values are what make the BMI and FFMI insights possible. Without them, those cards cannot be calculated.

How is the trend calculated, and how should I read predictions?

Your trend is a weighted moving average of your logged weights, which gives recent weigh-ins more influence while still smoothing out day-to-day swings from water, food and timing. It is designed to show the direction underneath the noise rather than the raw number on any single morning.

Weekly direction and projections only appear once there are enough entries spread over enough time for the estimate to mean anything. A projection extends your recent trend forward at its current rate — it assumes nothing changes, which rarely holds exactly. Treat it as an informational estimate, not a guarantee, and not medical advice.

What is included in Premium?

Premium unlocks the Insights tab: trend analysis, BMI and FFMI insights, expected weight development, and your projected goal date.

Logging weight, viewing your trend on the Weight tab, the full Logbook, and goal tracking are available without a subscription.

How do I restore a previous purchase?
  1. Make sure the device is signed in with the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.
  2. Open Settings and tap Upgrade to Premium, or open the Insights tab and tap Unlock Insights.
  3. On the purchase screen that appears, choose the restore option.

Restoring re-checks your Apple Account for an active subscription; it does not charge you again.

Premium did not unlock after I paid
  • Check that the device is online and signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.
  • Open Settings. If the Premium row shows “Subscription status unavailable”, tap it to retry the check.
  • Open the purchase screen again and use the restore option.
  • Close the app fully and reopen it.

If it is still locked, email support with the approximate date and time of purchase and the Apple Account region. Never send passwords or full payment card details.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions bought through the App Store are managed by Apple, not by this app. Cancellation has to be done through your Apple Account:

  1. Open the iOS Settings app.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions.
  3. Select the Weight Arc subscription.
  4. Choose the option to manage or cancel it.

You can also reach this screen from inside the app: when Premium is active, tapping the Premium row in Settings opens your Apple subscription settings.

Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues until the end of the period you have already paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple under Apple's own policies — the developer cannot cancel or refund an Apple subscription on your behalf.

I forgot my password
  1. On the sign-in screen, tap the forgotten-password link.
  2. Enter your email address to receive a 6-digit code.
  3. Enter the code and choose a new password of at least 6 characters.

If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder before requesting another code.

How do I delete my account and all my data?
  1. Open the Settings tab.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Under Danger Zone, tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm twice.

The app asks for confirmation twice because deletion is permanent. It removes your account, profile and every weight entry, and cannot be undone.

If you can no longer open the app, email support from the address connected to your account and ask for the account to be deleted.

Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription — cancel that separately in your Apple Account settings.

How do I report a bug?

Email support and include as much of the following as you can:

  • The app version.
  • Your iOS version.
  • Your iPhone model.
  • What you expected to happen.
  • What happened instead.
  • The exact steps that reproduce it.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if it helps and does not show anything you would rather keep private.
Where do I find the legal information?

The Privacy Policy explains what information is collected and how it is handled. The Terms of Use cover accounts, subscriptions and the conditions for using the app.
